Content Creator Full Strategy 

📌 About the Project
This project is an AI-generated infographic that explains the complete content creation workflow, from planning content ideas to building an audience and monetizing creative work.

It demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools can support content creators in producing engaging content, improving productivity, and developing a consistent online presence.

Key Highlights
* Plan: Choose a niche, understand your audience, and build a content calendar.
* Create: Record engaging videos using good lighting, different angles, and authentic storytelling.
* Edit: Improve footage with cuts, captions, effects, and audio.
* Publish: Optimize titles, thumbnails, hashtags, and posting schedules.
* Grow: Build a community, analyze performance, collaborate, and explore monetization opportunities.

AI Tools Featured
* ChatGPT — Ideas, scripts, captions, and research
* CapCut — Video editing and subtitles
* Canva — Thumbnails and graphics
* ElevenLabs — AI voiceovers
* Pika / Runway — AI video generation
* Notion — Content planning and tracking
* SocialPilot / Later — Social media scheduling and analytics

💡 Project Objective
To demonstrate how AI tools and a structured workflow can help content creators plan, produce, publish, and improve their content.
